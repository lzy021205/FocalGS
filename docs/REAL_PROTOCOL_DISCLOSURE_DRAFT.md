# Real-Data Protocol Disclosure Draft

Status: for coauthor and publication-policy review; not a paper correction by
itself.

The published protocol describes 15 viewpoints captured at five focal lengths,
with two viewpoints held out for testing. No archived Toy, Plant, or Bear data
version matching that statement exactly was found. The strongest recoverable
experiment lineages are:

| Scene | Capture/archive evidence | Registered and used | Actual split |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Toy | 96 source PNGs arranged as 16 apparent six-focal capture sequences; the sequence interpretation is inferred because raw capture records are unavailable | 90 | 72 / 18 |
| Plant | 90 CR3 originals arranged as 15 six-focal capture sequences | 76 | 71 / 5 |
| Bear | 96 CR3 originals arranged as 16 six-focal capture sequences | 68 | 64 / 4 |

Toy uses six focal settings at 24, 35, 50, 70, 85, and 105 mm. Plant and Bear
also retain six-setting acquisition evidence, but failed registrations make
their final split counts uneven; Plant additionally contains one 87 mm entry.
The available scripts, logs, configuration snapshots, rendered results, and
30,000-iteration checkpoints identify these versions more strongly than any
other local copy.

The authors should decide before data publication whether the difference needs
a publisher correction, an author note, or a clearly labeled supplemental data
statement. The release must describe these files as checkpoint-matched
paper-experiment archival candidates, not as a reconstructed 15-by-5 protocol.
The later strict Plant/Bear 24--85 mm benchmark should be offered only as a
separate post-paper benchmark.
