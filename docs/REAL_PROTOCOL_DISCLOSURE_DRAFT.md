# Real-Data Protocol Disclosure Draft

Status: provenance note for author and publication-policy review; not a paper
correction by itself.

The published protocol describes 15 viewpoints captured at five focal lengths,
with two viewpoints held out for testing. No archived Toy, Plant, or Bear data
version matching that statement exactly was found. The strongest recoverable
pre-paper experiment lineages contain:

| Scene | Capture/archive evidence | Registered and used | Actual split |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Toy | 96 source PNGs in apparent six-focal sequences | 90 | 72 / 18 |
| Plant | 90 CR3 originals in 15 six-focal sequences | 76 | 71 / 5 |
| Bear | 96 CR3 originals in 16 six-focal sequences | 68 | 64 / 4 |

The public dataset therefore does not claim to reproduce that paper protocol.
It publishes one post-paper strict 24--85 mm benchmark: Toy 60/15, Plant 58/14,
and Bear 57/13. Toy is produced by excluding only the 105 mm frames from its
historical split; Plant and Bear come from later clean strict splits. This
source difference is stated in the public metadata.

The authors should decide whether the paper/data difference requires a
publisher correction, an author note, or a supplemental statement. The public
benchmark must remain labeled post-paper and must not be presented as the
original paper-experiment dataset.
